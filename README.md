# Meridian MedAI
### By Shivam
**Intelligence for Better Healthcare.**

An AI-powered healthcare technology platform exploring the intersection of Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Medical Image Analysis, Generative AI and Multimodal Clinical Intelligence.

Meridian MedAI aims to bring multiple healthcare intelligence capabilities together in one modular platform, supporting the identification of potential disease-related abnormalities and early screening insights.

> **Important:** This project is intended for research, development and healthcare screening-support exploration. It is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis. AI-generated findings require appropriate clinical validation and professional review.

---

## Overview

Meridian MedAI is designed as an integrated healthcare AI ecosystem with multiple clinical intelligence modules.

The platform explores how Artificial Intelligence can assist in analysing medical images, interpreting clinical information, examining laboratory reports and identifying potential health-related abnormalities.

The long-term vision is to develop a unified, extensible architecture that can accommodate specialised AI models across different medical domains.

## Core Modules

### 1. Radiology & Medical Imaging AI
- Medical image upload and analysis workflows.
- X-ray, MRI and CT image analysis architecture.
- Abnormality detection and image segmentation.
- Computer Vision-based medical image processing.
- AI-assisted visualisation of potential regions of interest.

### 2. Oncology & Cancer Screening Support
- Exploration of skin lesion classification.
- Potential melanoma screening workflows.
- Image-based abnormality assessment.
- Architecture for integrating specialised cancer-related AI models.

### 3. Pathology & Laboratory Intelligence
- Laboratory report analysis workflows.
- Biomarker interpretation support.
- Longitudinal laboratory data visualisation.
- Clinical information summarisation.

### 4. ICU & Patient Monitoring Intelligence
- Patient vital-sign monitoring architecture.
- Heart rate and oxygen saturation tracking.
- Early-warning model integration.
- Patient risk-monitoring workflows.

### 5. Live Camera & Real-Time Computer Vision
- Live webcam access.
- Real-time camera feed.
- Image capture and preview.
- Camera switching and control.
- Live visual processing.
- Detection overlays where supported by an integrated model.
- Image preprocessing and model-inference integration.

### 6. Multimodal Clinical Intelligence
- Combining relevant medical images, symptoms and laboratory information.
- AI-assisted clinical information synthesis.
- Structured reporting workflows.
- Explainable AI integration.
- Clinician review and sign-off workflows.

## Architecture Vision

The long-term architecture follows a modular approach:

1. Frontend: User and clinician interfaces.
2. Backend: API orchestration and application logic.
3. Computer Vision: Medical image preprocessing and analysis.
4. AI/ML: Disease-specific model inference.
5. Generative AI: Clinical information synthesis.
6. Database: Patient records and screening history.
7. Explainability: Visual interpretation of supported model outputs.

Each clinical domain is intended to have independently developed and evaluated models rather than relying on one general-purpose AI model for every disease.

## Security, Privacy & Responsible AI

Healthcare information requires strong privacy and security safeguards.

Meridian MedAI aims to incorporate:
- Secure handling of medical information.
- Appropriate authentication and authorisation.
- Patient consent for image capture and processing.
- Controlled access to sensitive records.
- Secure API key management.
- Data minimisation.
- Clinical review of AI-generated findings.
- Transparent model limitations.

No AI-generated output should be treated as a confirmed medical diagnosis.

## Project Vision

The broader ambition behind Meridian MedAI is to explore how Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Generative AI and Multimodal Machine Learning can contribute to more accessible healthcare screening and clinical intelligence.

Rather than developing isolated disease-detection applications, the vision is to create a modular healthcare AI ecosystem capable of integrating specialised models across multiple clinical domains.
